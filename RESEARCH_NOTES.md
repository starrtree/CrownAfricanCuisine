# Research notes

## Strongest public signals

1. The menu is unusually broad: pizza, wings, burgers, sandwiches, rice plates, gyros and West African dishes.
2. Public reviews repeatedly mention wings, pizza, lamb over rice, freshness, portions and friendly service.
3. The restaurant's own recent social content puts Mix Over Rice in the spotlight.
4. The current site has messaging inconsistencies, including copy that references Kansas City even though the restaurant is in Cincinnati.
5. Forest Park has a large family/household audience and a meaningful foreign-born population, so the strongest broad positioning is not a narrow demographic stereotype. It is "a neighborhood restaurant where mixed groups can all find something they want."

## Audience strategy used

The redesign is built around behavioral segments rather than guessing sensitive attributes from reviewer names or profile photos:

- Families and mixed-preference groups
- Takeout/delivery customers
- Pizza/wings regulars who want a local alternative to chains
- Diners already familiar with West African food
- Curious diners who want an approachable entry point into dishes such as dibi, maffe, fufu and thiebou dienne

## Conversion strategy

- "Order online" is always visible.
- Familiar food imagery appears first so the site is immediately legible.
- Heritage dishes enter as the user scrolls, creating discovery without making the menu feel intimidating.
- Searchable menu lowers friction for repeat customers.
- Review themes reinforce the exact categories visitors are already responding to.
