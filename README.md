# Crown's Pizza & Wings — Cinematic Redesign Prototype

A scroll-driven redesign concept for Crown's Pizza & Wings & Best of African Cuisines in Cincinnati, Ohio.

## Design direction

The core idea is **"One Crown. Every Craving."** The restaurant is unusual because it can serve a mixed group without forcing everyone into one cuisine. The site makes that range feel intentional rather than random:

- familiar neighborhood comfort: pizza, wings, Phillys, burgers
- rice plates and gyros for fast, filling takeout
- West African / Senegalese-influenced dishes such as dibi, maffe, thiebou dienne, domoda and brochettes
- a family-friendly, casual neighborhood feel

The visual system comes directly from the physical restaurant and supplied assets: black-and-gold crown identity, warm amber walls, dark geometric wall panels, bright storefront signage, rich food photography, and the circular queen logo.

## Audience rationale

Forest Park is a diverse Cincinnati suburb with a substantial Black population and meaningful foreign-born population. The site therefore avoids treating African food as a niche side note. It presents the heritage menu as a primary part of the brand while keeping pizza/wings highly visible for local families, mixed groups, younger takeout customers and people discovering the restaurant through delivery/search.

Review themes surfaced from the restaurant's public site include:

- crisp wings + friendly service
- lamb over rice praised for tenderness, rice and fresh salad
- pizza praised as a satisfying local alternative to large chains

The restaurant's own social feed prominently highlights Mix Over Rice (chicken + lamb), reinforcing rice plates as a strong visual/marketing hook.

## Interaction patterns reused from StarrTree GitHub work

- GSAP + ScrollTrigger scroll choreography and pinned sequences, based on patterns used in the DIRTYBREAKFAST build
- reveal / parallax systems adapted from MaxStarrBio
- glass navigation, oversized editorial typography, cinematic image treatment and mobile-first CTA behavior consistent with StarrTree's current web design language

## Prototype features

- cinematic storefront hero
- scroll-reactive logo seal
- layered food-photo collage
- two-lane Comfort / Heritage story section
- pinned horizontal House Favorites rail on desktop
- searchable / filterable public menu
- direct Menufy ordering links
- research-backed review highlights
- location, phone and current public hours
- mobile sticky Order Online CTA
- reduced-motion fallback
